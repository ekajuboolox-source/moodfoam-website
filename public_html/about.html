About Us

Busujju Industries Ltd is a Ugandan manufacturing company producing high-quality mattresses under the Mood Foam brand. Our mission is to enhance sleep quality through innovative, comfortable, and durable mattresses tailored to diverse needs. With a focus on excellence, we combine advanced technology with sustainable practices to deliver products that improve lives. Our commitment to customer satisfaction drives us to continuously innovate and expand our offerings, ensuring every sleep experience is exceptional. Join us in transforming rest into rejuvenation with Mood Foam Mattresses. Mood Foam mattresses offer orthopedic support, durability, and customization. The company emphasizes affordability, timely delivery, and national pride. Through institutional partnerships and its "Rest for All" CSR program, Busujju promotes sustainable practices and social impact. It operates with a robust supply chain, professional workforce, and vision to be Uganda's most trusted mattress manufacturer.

Our Latest Blog

Sleep Science: Why Quality Sleep Should Be Your Priority

Sleep is not just rest; it's recovery. During deep sleep, your body repairs cells, consolidates memory, and strengthens your immune system. The right mattress plays a vital role in achieving this quality rest. At Mood Foam, our mattresses are designed to cradle your body and align your spine for optimal comfort.

Skimping on sleep can lead to fatigue, low focus, and even health issues like weight gain or depression. Invest in better sleep—because better nights lead to better days.

Orthopedic Comfort: Say Goodbye to Back Pain

Many people wake up feeling more tired or sore than when they went to bed. The culprit? Poor mattress support. Orthopedic mattresses, like our OrthoCare series, are crafted with rebonded foam that provides firm, stable support for your spine.

Whether you’re managing back pain or seeking preventive care, upgrading to orthopedic foam helps maintain natural alignment and reduces pressure points. Discover relief you can feel overnight.

Mattress Materials 101: Which One Is Right for You?

Every body is different—and so is every mattress. Foam mattresses are soft and contouring, while pocket spring mattresses provide bounce and airflow. Looking for durability and eco-friendliness? Try latex options.

At Mood Foam, we offer a range of materials so you can find what suits your sleep style. Side sleeper? Go plush. Back sleeper? Choose firm. Let us guide you to your perfect match.
