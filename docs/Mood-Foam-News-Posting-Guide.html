1. Logging in for the first time

  1. Open your welcome email. You will receive an email from Mood Foam Mattresses News with the subject about your new account. Check Spam or Promotions if you cannot see it.
  2. Click the link to set your password. Choose a long password you can remember (for example three or four unrelated words and a number). Save it in your phone's password manager.
  3. Go to the login page moodfoammattresses.com/updates/wp-admin/, type your username (or email) and your password, then press Log In.
  4. You are in. The screen you see is the Dashboard. You only need three things from the black menu on the left (or the menu icon on a phone): Posts, Media and Profile.

2. Write your first post

Do these steps in order. Each one takes under a minute.

  1. Start a new post. In the left menu click Posts, then Add New Post. If a welcome pop-up appears, close it with the X.
  2. Type the title where it says Add title. Make it clear and short: New Spring Mattresses Now Available. Aim for 40 to 60 characters. Avoid ALL CAPITALS and use at most one emoji.
  3. Write your message. Click the line under the title and type, or paste your text. Press Enter for a new paragraph. Short paragraphs of two or three sentences read best on phones.
  4. Make the first sentence count. It becomes the short preview on the news page and in Google. Say the main news straight away, for example "Our new orthopedic range is now available in Mityana, with free delivery this month."
  5. Add a heading (optional, for longer posts). Click the + button, choose Heading, then type. Use level H2 for main headings and H3 for smaller ones.
  6. Add a list (optional). Click + → List, type one item per line.
  7. Add a link (optional). Highlight some words, click the chain icon in the small toolbar, paste the address and press Enter. To invite WhatsApp chats, link to https://wa.me/256743053096.
Good to know

You will notice there are no colour or font-size options. That is on purpose: the website's design is applied automatically, so every post looks professional and is easy to read on every screen.

3. Photos: adding them and setting the cover

Prepare your photo first

ItemRecommended
Cover (Featured) photo1200 × 800 pixels, landscape (wider than tall), JPG, under 1 MB
Photos inside the postUp to 1200 pixels wide, JPG, under 1 MB each
Posters / flyersAllowed (tall is fine). Keep the important words away from the edges.

The website shrinks anything over 1600 pixels automatically, but smaller files upload faster on mobile data. The free tool squoosh.app or your phone's "resize" option does the job.

Add a photo inside the post

  1. Click the + button where you want the photo, choose Image.
  2. Click Upload and choose the photo from your phone or computer. (Already uploaded? Pick Media Library instead.)
  3. In the panel on the right, find Alt text and describe the photo in a few words, for example Queen size orthopedic mattress in the Mityana showroom. This helps Google and visually impaired visitors. If you leave it empty, the site uses your post title.
  4. Optional: type a caption under the photo. For several photos together choose + → Gallery.

Set the cover (Featured image): important

The cover photo is the picture shown on the news page, on the homepage and in WhatsApp and Facebook previews.

  1. Click the Settings icon in the top-right corner (a square with a panel on the right). Make sure the Post tab is selected.
  2. Open the Featured image section and click Set featured image.
  3. Choose a photo from the Media Library (or upload one), click it so it gets a blue border, then press Set featured image.
If you forget

The website uses the first photo in your post as the cover. Choose it yourself so you control which picture people see.

4. Finish and publish

Make the web address short

  1. In the Settings panel (Post tab) find the Link (or Permalink) row and click it.
  2. In the URL box type 3 to 5 words with hyphens, lowercase only, no spaces or emojis. Example: new-spring-mattresses

Write a short summary (Excerpt)

Still in the Settings panel, open Excerpt and write one or two sentences. This is the text visitors see under the title on the news page and often what Google shows. If you leave it empty, the first sentences of the post are used.

Preview, then publish

  1. Click Preview at the top and choose Preview in new tab. Read it once and check the photos.
  2. Come back and press the blue Publish button at the top right. A panel asks you to confirm: press Publish again.
  3. Click View Post to see it live. Done.
It is not instant everywhere

Your post is on the news page straight away. The homepage and some browsers can take up to 5 minutes to refresh. Google usually needs a few hours to a few days to list a new post.

7. Do and don't

Do

  • Post regularly. One or two posts a week is better than ten in one day.
  • Use real photos of your products and showroom.
  • Mention prices, sizes, delivery areas and offer end-dates clearly.
  • Preview before publishing.
  • Log out on shared computers.

Don't

  • Do not click Update on WordPress, themes or plugins if a notice appears. Boolox handles those.
  • Do not change Settings, Appearance or Plugins.
  • Do not copy photos or text from other websites (copyright, and Google penalises copies).
  • Do not publish customers' private details.
  • Do not share your password.

8. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
I cannot log inCheck Caps Lock, then use Lost your password?. After several wrong attempts the site blocks you for a while for safety; wait 15 minutes and try again.
My post is not on the homepage yetWait up to 5 minutes and refresh. Only the 3 newest posts show on the homepage; all posts show on the news page.
The cover photo looks cut offThe news cards are wide. Use a landscape cover (1200 × 800) and keep the main subject in the centre.
The page looks different from what I expectedUse Preview. The website controls colours and fonts so posts always look consistent.
I deleted something by mistakeOpen Posts → All Posts → Trash and press Restore. Posts stay in the Trash for 30 days.
Photo will not uploadUse a JPG or PNG under 5 MB. Check your internet connection and try again.
